## Regression and valid quality claims
fit prepends intercept, calculates weighted ridge inverse (X'WX + I for l2; otherwise X'WX), and weighted coefficient estimate Phi_hat. Stored s_2 is weighted residual quadratic plus Phi_hat'Phi_hat, divided by N; this expression includes the coefficient penalty in either l2 branch. predict returns linear mean and predictive standard deviation derived from s_2 times (I + augmented-X V_Phi augmented-X transpose); it includes observation noise, not only coefficient uncertainty.

get_creds(percent=95, n_samples=10000, get_intercept=False) samples inverse-gamma variance then multivariate-normal coefficients and returns a per-coefficient percentile of absolute deviations from coefficient means. These are unsigned marginal radii, not signed endpoints or a joint region. A defensible score can summarize this fitted surrogate posterior on h_B only (e.g. explicitly normalized marginal coefficient radii, or predictive standard deviation at specified points). State normalization, kernel/perturbation/regularization dependence and interpretation as narrower modeled uncertainty only. It is not classifier uncertainty, fidelity/local loss, calibrated quality, equivalence, or a certificate. Fidelity requires separately measured black-box-versus-surrogate discrepancy.

## Parent mathematical scope check
Native predict returns sqrt(s2*(1+x_aug V x_aug)). Under the code's IG(N/2,N*s2/2) sigma2 sampler this is predictive Student-t scale, not full marginal standard deviation; the latter needs factor sqrt(N/(N-2)) for N>2 under the model. Stored native_predictive_std is preserved as an API diagnostic name/value, not a verified fidelity-error standard deviation. No correction to native source or output has been made.
