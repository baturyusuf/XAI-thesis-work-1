## Theorem and derivation

VERIFIED primary Theorem 1 (sections 1.1 and 2.1): fixed finite candidate grid, null H_j:R(lambda_j)>alpha, P_cal(p_j<=u)<=u under every true null for all u in [0,1], and FWER control at delta give P_cal(sup over lambda in Lambda_hat R(lambda)<=alpha)>=1-delta. The paper sets sup(empty)=-infinity; this does NOT certify an available policy. Outer probability is over calibration sampling and legitimate measurement randomness, conditional on independently fixed fitting artifacts, policy family and target law. R is population risk; within the accepted subpopulation is a different conditioning. Section 5 warns of marginal, not arbitrary subgroup or individual, guarantees. Same-sample choice INSIDE certified Lambda_hat is allowed, but fitting new candidates or order on the same testing sample is not. Appendix D uses an independent graph-selection split for learned order.

VERIFIED primary sections 2.2--2.3: Bonferroni tests each of N at delta/N without p-value independence; a preordered one-start fixed sequence tests at full delta and stops on first non-rejection. The first true null bounds the FWER. Proposition 1 HB bounded [0,1] p-value is min(exp(-n KL(mean(T) wedge alpha, alpha)), e P(Bin(n,alpha)<=ceil(n mean(T)))); clipping at 1 is conservative. Section 3.2 uses an exact binomial tail on accepted binary losses (fixed selector, iid observations, conditioning on accepted count), not fractional losses. Section 3.3 discusses finite-sample selective MSE but does not make unbounded square losses HB-bounded: an actual finite-sample tail assumption or bound must be justified. Appendix B Proposition B.1 finite mean/variance CLT p-values are asymptotic, not finite-sample exact.

VERIFIED independent algebra: for fixed A in {0,1}, 0<=L<=1, 0<alpha<1 and C=P(A=1)>0, define R=E[L|A=1], T=alpha+A(L-alpha). When A=0 T=alpha; when A=1 T=L: 0<=T<=1. E[T]-alpha=E[A(L-alpha)]=C(R-alpha). Hence R>alpha iff E[T]>alpha (also equality at the boundary). At C=0, R is undefined, not zero risk. E[AL]=CR, not R. Acceptance, score and loss target must be fixed independent of the testing data.

VERIFIED separate simultaneous coverage derivation: iid base-unit Chat=mean(A_i) obeys P(C<Chat-r)<=exp(-2nr^2). For m predeclared policies, r=sqrt(log(m/delta_c)/(2n)) makes all m lower bounds max(0,Chat-r) simultaneous with confidence >=1-delta_c. A positive floor cmin needs lower>=cmin; this also ensures C>0 on the good event. Risk FWER delta_r and coverage delta_c require a union bound, joint confidence >=1-delta_r-delta_c. Multiple predeclared families consume further union budget. Fixed-sequence risk does not remove simultaneous coverage correction.

VERIFIED conditional MC identity under its assumptions: bounded Lhat with E[Lhat|base observation, explanation, acceptance, score-relevant randomness]=L_exact gives E[A Lhat]=E[A L_exact]; marginally unbiased probes need not suffice when the acceptance score reads their noise. Probe IDs cannot prove this condition. Repeated probes are one base unit, not extra iid n. Cluster splitting can prevent role leakage but cannot make correlated calibration rows iid; bootstrap cannot repair the original finite-sample theorem. A new justified iid cluster-level sampling unit and target, or a separately established dependent-data test, is required.
