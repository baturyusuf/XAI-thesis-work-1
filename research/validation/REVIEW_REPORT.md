## 6. H2 Risk-Coverage Audit
Population accepted risk E[AL]/E[A], empirical accepted mean, calibration estimate and final-test estimate are distinct. At zero acceptance risk is None, independently checked. Probes estimate one base-unit loss, not extra observations. Different seeds/streams do not prove independence.

Prefix AURC=(1/n)sum_k cumulative_loss(k)/k. Tied losses (0,1) give 1/4 or 3/4; uniform-permutation expectation 1/2. For q=(0,1,1), losses (0,0,1), expected prefix area 7/36 differs from endpoint-block area 2/9. General expected block contribution is (1/n)sum_r(S+r*mu)/(b+r); exact permutation checks agree. Same IDs+seed is reproducible under input permutation but not arbitrary seed/ID/tie-order invariant. Fixed-coverage partial ties require explicit randomization; threshold policies cannot generally attain interior block counts.

Outcome-chosen seeds/IDs or tie ordering can systematically favor a method. Expected ties remove that ordering noise but do not make all tie/coverage policies equivalent or eliminate score-quantization effects. No primary tie metric selected.

Clipping example independently confirms raw loss=invalid mass=1/4, clipped loss=0. Projection mechanically hides raw error; native_probability rejects invalid values and clip_adapter is a changed explanation target. Raw residual may exceed one and cannot inherit clipped bounded-risk guarantees. The loss contract must name f/class/output, surrogate, law, range and independent measurement. ## 7. LTT Statistical Guarantee Audit
Primary: Learn then Test, https://arxiv.org/html/2110.01052v5, Theorem 1/Proposition 1, sections 1.1/2/3.2/3.3/5, Appendices B/C/D directly accessed. Superuniform null p-values plus FWER delta imply P_cal(all certified risks<=alpha)>=1-delta. Probability is over calibration sampling (and justified per-unit measurement randomness), conditional on independently fixed fitting artifacts and family. It is not a per-person/subgroup or arbitrary-shift guarantee.

For 0<=L<=1, A in {0,1}, 0<alpha<1 and C=E[A]>0, T=alpha+A(L-alpha) is either alpha or L, hence bounded. E[T]-alpha=C*(E[L|A=1]-alpha). This independently proves the accepted-risk null reduction, NOT a new contribution. At C=0 risk is undefined.

Hoeffding and primary HB are finite-sample under their IID bounded-loss conditions; accepted-only exact binomial requires binary loss/appropriate conditioning; CLT is asymptotic, not a finite-sample unbounded-loss exemption. Bonferroni allows dependent p-values across fixed policies. Fixed sequence needs a predetermined order and stop at first non-rejection; learning order needs a genuinely independent split. Choosing within the certified set is not permission to fit scores/families on calibration.

Coverage lower bound max(0,Chat-sqrt(log(M/delta_C)/(2n))) is simultaneous for M fixed policies under IID. Positive floor is separate from risk FWER; joint error <=delta_R+delta_C, with across-family/controller budgets also accounted. Six pilot families use risk/coverage 1/120 each, union total <=1/10; source inspection and reproduced outputs show no risk null rejected. One candidate low coverage does not make coverage the sole cause of all six abstentions.

Independent fixed-budget bounded loss estimates preserve latent expected loss if conditionally unbiased given observation/explanation/acceptance and score-relevant randomness. Marginal unbiasedness or distinct stream IDs is not sufficient. Cluster split/bootstrap does not establish row-IID LTT; alternative justified sampling/theory needed. See LTT_GUARANTEE_AUDIT.md.
