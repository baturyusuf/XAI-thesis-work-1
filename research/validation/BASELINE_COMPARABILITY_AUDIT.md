2026-10-22. Sources A1 AGENT4_BASELINE_INTEGRATION/INTEGRATION_MATRIX/AGENT3_BENCHMARK_REQUIREMENTS and A3 BASELINE_ELIGIBILITY/COST_AND_QUERY_BUDGET. These pins predate Agent4 delivery; no claim about its current progress.

H1 comparisons must match frozen predictor, declared probability/class/label property, logical groups and Q/U. PAXp probabilistic precision, SIS replacement threshold, RDE continuous-mask distortion and VeriX pointwise certificate are different native objectives. Native and adapted arms must be named separately; same support does not imply same masses. Certificate validity may transfer to any within-U law, not outside U. Exact enumeration is a privileged small-domain referee, not a cheap scalable competitor.

H2 compares scores on the SAME frozen E/f/class/law/functional loss and total permitted information. Direct independent probe, learned functional-loss score and eligible Bayes local-error score + existing LTT are strong null candidates, not new algorithms. Bayes posterior uncertainty is not automatically a calibrated functional-risk score; target mismatch must be disclosed. Prediction confidence/random accept/always explain are controls, not sufficient strong competitors.

Attribution top-k conversion is an adapter, rule precision is not surrogate residual, native Selective Explanations includes fallback/mixture semantics, CRC expected-risk and LTT high-probability accepted-risk are not interchangeable. ReCalX calibrated g and original f are different probability targets; SST changes the predictor and training costs, so use a separate intrinsic arm.

Cost ledger must include logical requests, uncached rows, batches, gradients/solver work, sampler fit/rejections, score-label generation/training, calibration, online and evaluator work, cold/warm runtime and memory. Offer equivalent cache/reuse opportunities without evaluator leakage. Count all attempts, retries/timeouts and unsupported methods; no numerical zero penalty or omitted failures. Current kernel is not a complete runtime/cost profiler.
