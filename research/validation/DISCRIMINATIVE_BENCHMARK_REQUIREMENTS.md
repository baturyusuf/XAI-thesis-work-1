## Decision question

Phase 1 is a limited synthetic smoke test. Its direct, learned, random, and reversed score families all accepted the same 64 evaluation units at full coverage with the same observed exact-point risk. This is a nondiscriminating pilot setting, not evidence of algorithm adequacy, superiority, or necessity. Future work should ask: does a predeclared candidate improve selective risk/coverage or the complete cost-risk tradeoff, practically and reproducibly, against strong existing methods and difficult nulls under matched budgets and calibrated uncertainty?

## Design requirements

1. **Heterogeneous fidelity targets.** Predefine distinct estimands: local functional/predictive error under a declared perturbation law; coefficient/attribution recovery where a known generating explanation exists; and stability/robustness only with an explicit estimand. State target/output scale, preprocessing, neighborhood/evaluation law, loss and clipping, and inference unit. Include easy and difficult regimes varying dimension, signal/noise, correlation, sparsity, boundaries, sample size and curvature. Do not substitute weighted RÂ², coefficient similarity, posterior scale, or finite-point error for population fidelity.

2. **Nonlinearity, interactions and sampler misspecification.** Include additive nonlinear and interaction-dominated functions (including interactions absent from an additive explainer), justified localized/discontinuous structure, correlated/heterogeneous inputs and varying curvature. Vary explanation sampling law against the common target/evaluation law: correctly specified and predeclared misspecified/shifted samplers. Evaluate all methods against the same target distribution; use analytic or independently validated references where possible and label computational-oracle-only cases.

3. **Strong baselines and hard nulls.** Include correctly implemented/tuned direct-query selective methods, learned local-error scores with their B-label acquisition and fitting fully charged, and compatible native LIME/Bayesian local-uncertainty comparators only where estimands/assumptions match. Include strong direct/learned/Bayes local-error null regimes where established methods should match or beat the candidate; no extra labels, target information or compute for the candidate. Diagnostic controls: random, reversed, constant, label/feature permutation where valid, deliberately misspecified sampler, and an oracle ranking clearly marked nondeployable. State expected control behavior before running.

4. **Same-coverage risk, AURC and threshold policy are distinct.** Report risk at matched coverage on a prespecified coverage grid, full risk-coverage curves/AURC under one explicit tie convention, and calibrated-threshold policy outcomes separately (selected threshold, achieved coverage, test risk, uncertainty). Do not conflate prefix AURC, interpolated area, fixed-coverage risk, and threshold-selected risk. Use common IDs, loss observations and tie resolution; include target-coverage failures. No post hoc threshold or AURC convention may break a tie.

5. **Paired, complete total cost.** Pair tasks/instances/seeds when valid and predeclare pairing unit. Charge explanation samples and generation, predictor calls and rows, B/fit labels, calibration and evaluation labels, exact referees, fitting/tuning/inference, preprocessing, retries/failures, caching and setup as applicable. Publish end-to-end standalone costs and shared-cost allocations with a fixed amortization rule. Match complete pipeline budgets or prespecify a cost-risk frontier; never compare inference-only learned cost with all-in direct cost. Report repeated wall-time measurements with disjoint timing buckets, hardware/environment, concurrency, memory and uncertainty. `NOT_MEASURED` is unknown, not zero.

6. **Positive and negative controls.** Negative: random, reversed, constant, valid permutation controls and sampler/target misspecification. Positive: exact/reference calculations on small cases, a known oracle score where mathematically available (nondeployable), and a regime where the candidateâ€™s asserted mechanism should succeed. Define expected behavior and pass/fail criteria in advance. Controls diagnose the benchmark; they do not replace power or external validity.

## Concrete candidates for Agent 3 review (not executed)

- A fixed low-dimensional factorial suite can combine: known affine reference targets; additive curvature with prespecified curvature strata; XOR/product or parity interactions; a boundary/kink model; correlated inputs with conditional-versus-marginal sampler mismatch. Set parameter grids from mechanism definitions and independent analytic anchors, not from which regime favors an algorithm. Add off-support rejection separately from fidelity failure. Native Ridge shrinkage means an affine predictor alone need not give exactly zero error: a supplied exact affine explanation is a separate positive control.
- Include an explicit always-accept controller/null and calibrated direct-probe, B-only functional-loss and Bayesian local-error alternatives. For Bayes, first approve a native same-object score contract: posterior uncertainty is not measured functional error, and h_B posterior cannot be transferred to h_L. Either compare scores for the same explanation object or treat differing banks as separate end-to-end pipelines with separate interpretations.
- Primary candidates are paired accepted-risk difference at prespecified common coverage, or coverage gain at fixed risk with certification failures retained; neither is selected here. Prefix/expected-tie AURC are secondary candidates requiring a tie contract, not certified thresholds. Include noncertification and unattainable requested coverage in the estimand, not as silent exclusions.
- For practical effects, propose an absolute bounded-loss margin epsilon_L derived from the largest tolerated additional selected-class probability error; a coverage margin epsilon_C derived from how many additional units can receive acceptable explanations; and a cost ratio R justified by a deployment compute/SLA requirement. Use an independently chosen stricter equivalence tolerance, report paired confidence bounds against both margins, and require precision well below the chosen margin. Where no application utility exists, label conclusions synthetic-only and have Agent 3/human justify margins from prespecified oracle-separated difficulty regimes before generation. No numerical value is adopted from this pilot.
- Prospective power must use the paired unit-level variance and independent task/mechanism replication (not perturbations as extra n), with a fixed maximum budget and stopping rule. Include null/boundary calibration operating-characteristic checks and the possibility of NO_POLICY_CERTIFIED without calling it a new-algorithm gap.

## Statistical safeguards

- Define independent units, clusters, split roles and conditional sampling assumptions; keep training/score fitting, calibration and final evaluation separate. Repeated probes within a unit are not extra independent units.
- Prespecify multiplicity over methods, tasks, metrics and thresholds; use simultaneous uncertainty or controlled familywise inference. Preserve the calibration/test boundary.
- Perform prospective power/precision analysis for primary paired estimands, plausible variance/correlation, clustering, multiplicity and attrition. Underpowered nulls do not establish equivalence.
- Before data generation/inspection, independently justify a practical-superiority margin and a separate equivalence/noninferiority margin from domain consequences, not Phase 1â€™s observed gap. Report intervals relative to both. Do not tune margins, thresholds, seeds or stopping on outcomes.
- Check type-I error, interval coverage, calibration and operating characteristics using independent references across null, alternative, boundary, misspecification and failure cases. State untestable theorem assumptions.
- Freeze code/config, seed policy, exclusions, failure handling, stopping, comparison family and reporting plan. Separate exploratory development from confirmatory evaluation.

## Falsification: when a new algorithm is not justified

The claimed need is not supported if a strong direct, learned or compatible Bayesian/local-error baseline is equivalent within the independently chosen margin; candidate benefit vanishes at matched coverage or under a valid common tie rule; it misses the practical improvement margin; extra full-pipeline cost or privileged information erases its advantage; negative controls perform similarly; positive controls fail; gains do not replicate across predeclared regimes; or uncertainty is too wide or miscalibrated to distinguish benefit from equivalence. Inconclusive/underpowered is not superiority and not novelty. If established methods meet the target at comparable total cost, prefer them absent a separately justified unmet need.

This design fixes none of those numerical choices. No experiment, real/private/hidden data, package installation, threshold freeze, algorithm proposal or novelty assertion is authorized. Phase 1 establishes neither population-risk validity nor method superiority nor a need for a new method.


## Precision sizing note (design mathematics, not a new result)

For a prespecified fixed bounded-loss mean under IID assumptions, a distribution-free two-sided Hoeffding radius is sqrt(log(2/eta)/(2*n)); simultaneous m-mean precision can use sqrt(log(2*m/eta)/(2*n)). Therefore a desired radius epsilon requires at least log(2*m/eta)/(2*epsilon^2) independent units under this conservative design bound. Observed min/max loss is not a valid universal range to plug in after the fact. Agent 3 should use prospective paired-variance/power calculations where justified; no new test-risk confidence interval, effect margin or sample size is frozen here. Sixty-four realized units and one mechanism do not establish cross-mechanism equivalence.
