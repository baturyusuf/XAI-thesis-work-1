## LTT priority gates

Separate simultaneous coverage bound on n from accepted-risk test on K; risk/coverage budgets and across-score/controller selection consume declared total budget. A controller selected after calibration cannot inherit a single unadjusted guarantee.

Freeze both controller variants, policies, direction, alpha and thresholds independently of calibration. Use identical base observations, fixed measured loss/probes, family and minimum coverage for a paired diagnostic; if jointly certifying/selecting across variants, correct across both. Disclose any power/gap differences and exact versus MC loss. A bounded unbiased probe mean needs conditional unbiasedness given score/acceptance randomness, not only marginal unbiasedness.
