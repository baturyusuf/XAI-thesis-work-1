## 8. H2 Conditional Risk and FWER

[Statistical audit](PHASE1_H2_STATISTICAL_AUDIT.md) derives conditioning on random accepted count K: fixed policy/independent B gives IID accepted losses conditional on K>0; conditional unbiasedness transfers their mean to accepted population clipped risk. K=0 means p=1 and risk undefined. Coverage is separately bounded on 64 base units; perturbations do not enlarge n.

Four fixed families x (risk 1/80 + coverage 1/80) = **1/10**. Each of five thresholds gets risk 1/400 and coverage 1/400. Bonferroni requires valid constituent tests, not independent families. Choosing maximum certified coverage is allowed under simultaneous bounds. The >=.9 ideal-IID calibration statement concerns risk <=.2 and positive coverage >=.05; it is not a test-sample promise, individual guarantee or proof of IID from seeds. No C/T leakage found in inspected B-only fitting path; unlogged historical prespecification is not proved.
