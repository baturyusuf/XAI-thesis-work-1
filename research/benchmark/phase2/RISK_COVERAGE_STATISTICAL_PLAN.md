# Phase 2 risk–coverage/statistical plan — candidates only

No primary metric or confirmatory parameters are frozen. Existing accepted-count HB is a strong null, not a new contribution. Scope is declared bounded functional loss, not universal XAI quality. Source/theorem premises stay conditional; this REVIEW runs no benchmark.

## Estimands

Condition on independently fitted predictor/explainer/score and G-built finite family. U includes explanation and acceptance randomness. A_t=1[q(U)<=t], c_t=P(A_t=1), R_t=E[A_t L(U)]/c_t for c_t>0; risk undefined at zero. Calibration Lhat in [0,1] must obey E[Lhat|U,B,G]=L(U). Independent loss probes are sufficient; marginal unbiasedness alone does not protect acceptance-conditioned risk. Shared outcomes across scores may correlate tests, not invalidate Bonferroni; within-unit probes never enlarge n.

Conditional on K=k>0 for a FIXED policy, accepted losses are IID from Lhat|A=1. Apply accepted-count HB; mix over K, with p=1 at K0. This reasoning fails for a policy family fitted on C without valid correction. B/G conditioning is allowed; fixed hashes/IDs do not prove IID. No dependence/shift/subgroup/individual guarantee is inferred.

## Multiplicity and policy selection proposal

Registry cap17 individually named bank/scorer/one-controller families (L01-L08/B01-B09) jointly over the two native banks for ONE mechanism/law/resource cell, threshold cap9 each. Per-cell/per-replication joint delta=.1, risk half=.05/coverage half=.05. Per family each budget=1/340; per threshold each event budget=1/3060. Total17*9*2/3060=.1. Reserved/ineligible slots remain unspent; changing family/controller count requires a new pre-C budget. No assumption of independent families.

Coverage LCB=max(0,K/n-sqrt(log(3060)/(2*n))). Eligibility requires risk rejection AND LCB>=c_min>0 with the declared conservative numeric guard. Select max certified calibration coverage, first sorted threshold on ties. Report all p-values/risk/coverage reasons and thresholds, not only successes. Always-accept is tested, not automatically safe. Threshold family comes from B/G only. Familywise guarantee is per registered cell/replication, NOT simultaneous over all cells, horizons, repetitions or later choices; a studywide guarantee must allocate delta across them before outcomes. Calibration delta and scientific comparison alpha are different budgets.

## Primary candidates for human choice

1. **Coverage delivered by a conditionally certified selected policy at fixed alpha and total cost.** Record calibration LCB and independent T coverage separately. Across independent repetitions, average T coverage of certified output; NO_POLICY gives delivered coverage0 but risk remains undefined, not risk0. Report abstention/BLOCKED/errors separately and never hide them in a complete-case mean. Safety audit is a co-requirement, not traded off against coverage in a weighted score.
2. **Independent risk at prespecified matched coverage.** Compare on paired units only when both policies achieve the required coverage under an approved development/calibration rule. Report shortfall and unattainability; do not force exact T rank counts then claim a deployed threshold. Differences in risk are differences of ratios if acceptance masks differ.
3. **Complete cost–coverage frontier at fixed risk.** Fixed horizon/cost allocation; no inference-only versus all-in cost comparison. Equivalent/adequate existing frontier falsifies a need claim.

Recommendation is candidate1 with independent safety/cost reporting; NOT final selection. Raw numeric score thresholds from Phase1 are not reused as optimal Phase2 thresholds. Candidate2/3 are alternatives or secondary families, not later promoted by outcomes.

## Descriptive/selective diagnostics

Risk–coverage curves, prefix AURC, expected-tie AURC, block endpoints, fixed-rank coverage and calibration-selected thresholds have different estimands. AURC is not a risk guarantee. Proposed tie-neutral descriptive default: inherited expected_tie_aurc, plus threshold-achievable complete-block endpoints; ordinary seeded prefix realization may be supplementary. For block size m, preceding count b/loss S, block mean mu, contribution is (1/n)*sum_(r=1..m)(S+r*mu)/(b+r). All-tied controls must be invariant to input permutations under that metric.

A partial tied block cannot be implemented by a deterministic score threshold. If randomized tie acceptance is desired, its independent U and probabilities must be part of a NEW fixed family/multiplicity contract; no outcome-ordered ties. Keep proposed tie convention open for approval.

Oracle regret only relative to declared oracle class/law. M1/M4 deterministic G-only feasible coverage2/3 differs from randomized true-loss optimum3/4 at the alpha boundary. Native M2 full oracle frontier is unknown; context gate.5 is feasible, not claimed optimal. A true-loss/population oracle is privileged and not a certified deployed policy. Empirical finite-T oracle regret is a separate descriptive quantity, never population truth.

## Evaluation, pairing and scientific inference

Freeze policies before independent T. Report numerator, denominator, K, conditional risk, coverage and simultaneous uncertainty; T outcomes never refit scores/grids. Conditional accepted-count bounded CIs use K, not n or n*probes; coverage admits binomial bounds under IID. Zero K stays undefined and interval uninformative. Multiple policies/points need simultaneous rather than pointwise bands. Continuous referee errors propagate to risk intervals/decisions; use validated bounds near alpha, not an unqualified numerical mean.

Pair score methods on the same h/center/T/referee data. Pair native banks by base units but label end-to-end estimand. Independent full simulation repetitions regenerate B/G/C/T and explainer randomness. Holding B fixed estimates a different conditional operating characteristic; do not mix them. Use paired repetition-level contrasts/intervals; unit-level bootstraps cannot pretend to replicate B/controller randomness. Recompute ratio denominators in resampling; report zero-accept replicates instead of discarding them. No calibration bootstrapping repairs violated IID premises.

Prespecify scientific comparison family and Holm/simultaneous CIs separately from LTT. Fixed mechanism cells are not independent real datasets; generalization is to the stated designed regimes. Effect/equivalence margins require independent utility/power justification; nonsignificance is not equivalence. No optional stopping/favorable seeds/exclusions. Power, repetition counts and failure rules: POWER_AND_EFFECT_PLANNING and FALSIFICATION_AND_NEGATIVE_CONTROLS.

## Validity-frequency target

For M1/M4, integrate true population R/c conditional on each frozen score family: exact G/W/binomial sums distinguish actual unsafe certified policies from noisy T estimates. Count an event if ANY certified policy violates its risk/coverage statement in a repetition (matching the simultaneous guarantee), and report selected-policy violations separately. No-certificate repetitions are not errors, and are reported separately from denominators restricted to issued certificates.

For native h randomness/Q_J, exact unit quadrature is NOT full population truth. Unless independent integration bounds resolve the event, label violation status unresolved, report lower/upper frequency bounds and do not drop ambiguous trials. T empirical risk above alpha alone is not a theorem violation. Operating characteristics need independent repeated experiments, NOT executed in this REVIEW.
