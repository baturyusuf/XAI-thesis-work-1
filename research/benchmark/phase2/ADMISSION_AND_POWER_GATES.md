## 2. Separate admission questions

1. Reference question: is always-accept provably unsafe AND a positive useful feasible policy known? Otherwise NO_SAFE_FRONTIER/ALL_SAFE or UNRESOLVED, not scorer failure.
2. Information question: does THIS registered score/policy family contain such a policy? M4 m1 sample-mean thresholds do not; W/context and analytic alternatives do. More C cannot change that family frontier.
3. Grid question: is the useful member included by a B/G-only grid BEFORE C? If not, GRID_APPROXIMATION, no post-C threshold repair. Include context q=C and direct q0 explicitly, not by hoping quantiles find them.
4. Calibration question: can risk AND coverage gates plausibly pass at the fixed C budget? If not, CALIBRATION_POWER/RESOURCE_LIMIT, not information insufficiency. An easy all-safe target may hide ranking differences; inspect predeclared population reference, not just endpoint certifications.
5. Native question: are high/low losses natural outcomes of unchanged native generation? M2 has structural bounds; its realized distribution still requires approved validation. M4 is supplied-object reference regardless of native crossing.
6. Scientific question: are practical margins, paired uncertainty and resource scope approved? Without them, no superiority/equivalence/necessity claim, even if all code checks pass.
