# Falsification, controls and failure attribution — proposed

H2 is an open question, not a novelty claim. No candidate new algorithm exists in this review. All outcomes, including existing-method adequacy, remain reportable. Reference/native/assumption-violation controls must not be pooled as one algorithm leaderboard.

## Falsification/adequacy outcomes retained

- Direct independent probes plus accepted-count LTT meet approved risk/coverage at matched complete cost, including public finite-Q exact enumeration when affordable.
- B-only functional-loss learning is equivalent/noninferior or better once generation/labels/tuning/calibration and workload horizon are charged.
- A source-compatible native Bayes local-error proxy already supplies any claimed uncertainty advantage on its OWN h_B bank; no transfer to h_L.
- Context-only or always-accept suffices in the declared target; no need for selective innovation in that setting. Always-accept unsafe in M1/M2/M4 is a derived mechanism condition, not an excuse to exclude all-safe regimes.
- Apparent failure disappears with correct Q, an appropriate existing score model, adequate preplanned C/power, or a reasonable budget. Sampler/measurement/power mistakes are not a new-XAI gap.
- Native methods remain reliable across predeclared difficult conditions within practical margins. Nonsignificance/underpower does not establish equivalence or a gap.

Only a reproducible, practically meaningful residual limitation against strong correctly applied baselines at fair information/target/full cost warrants Agent1 novelty re-review. Even that is not automatic novelty: search literature/mechanism and competing solutions again. Do not remove null-success cells or select the metric favoring a future idea.

## Negative/diagnostic controls

- Independent random score: in M1/M2/M4, every positive-coverage fixed random threshold has the unsafe population mean of always-accept. Rare false certification is governed by error allocation, not guaranteed impossible in every replication.
- Reversed exact direct score: deliberately poor ordering; with noisy probes, quantify its actual oracle risk rather than assuming strict reversal at every unit. Its direct probe cost is not0.
- Constant score/always endpoint: ties test threshold endpoints and expected-tie AURC; no outcome-ordered IDs/seeds. Input permutation must preserve the chosen tie-neutral diagnostic.
- Clipping counterexample p=[0,0,1,1], h=[-1,0,1,1]: raw loss/invalid rate1/4, clipped loss0. This tests object identity, not natural native failure. Record raw/clipped/invalid/gain everywhere.
- M3 diagonal/product law: same h, losses0 versus.225. Distinct estimands, not algorithm deterioration under one target. Off-support predictor remains explicit.
- Same-probe acceptance/loss or deliberate biased calibration: mark assumption-invalid negative controls and BLOCK guarantee; never count them as failures of a valid LTT theorem. No such invalid experiment is authorized here.
- Valid label/feature permutation controls only after specifying what symmetry preserves the target. Arbitrary permutation can change the problem, so not a universal requirement.

## Diagnostic decision tree for any future FAIL

1. Source/API/native-conformance, predictor probability, scaler/intercept/order and adapter checks fail -> IMPLEMENTATION/INTEGRATION, not scientific algorithm failure.
2. Actual loss/object/Q differs -> TARGET_OR_SAMPLER_MISMATCH; if intentionally varied, compare as a different estimand.
3. Oracle/reference has no useful safe coverage -> NO_FEASIBLE_FRONTIER for that cell, not score failure; retain as stress scope, not selective superiority evidence.
4. A good fixed policy exists but even reference cannot certify under the C budget -> CALIBRATION_RISK_POWER or COVERAGE_POWER, not a new method gap.
5. Exact-loss selector works but the permitted m1 sample-mean/global-threshold family has no safe policy -> SCORE_POLICY_CLASS_LIMIT; compare stronger existing m4/learned methods before generalizing.
6. Score has useful attainable policies but G-grid lacks them -> GRID_APPROXIMATION, with no post-C threshold repair.
7. Deadline/budget/availability failure -> RESOURCE_LIMIT with costs and all attempts, not fabricated loss1/zero-cost success.
8. Proper native/score/power/cost checks pass yet approved practical target fails -> possible METHOD_LIMITATION, still INCONCLUSIVE about novelty until stronger methods/literature audit.

Freeze control expectations before outcomes; preserve noncertification and ambiguous referee events. Distinguish explanatory cause from inferred causality: overlapping diagnostics may require further approved tests. No new experiments were run.

## Revision admission and null-success policy

The proposed pre-outcome structural gates in ADMISSION_AND_POWER_GATES.md are not outcome-driven filters. Retain mechanism versions/all-attempt records even when an existing method solves them. M2 context-only and public exact/analytic/quadrature direct are mandatory eligible strong nulls; public continuous law alone is not proof of costly evaluation. M4 W-only safe selection and analytic observable-information alternatives remain eligible; the m1 threshold exclusion is not a universal one-probe lower bound. Supplied M1/M4 explainers are intentional controls, never evidence that native LIME failed.

If random/reversed certify in an analytically unsafe independent-random reference, distinguish a valid error-budget event from implementation/assumption failure; do not forbid every rare false rejection by fiat. If every competent score succeeds, report a non-discriminating comparative setting/existing-method adequacy, not a reason to hide easy strata. M2 high/low structure arises from affine interaction, not corrupting fitted explanations; its native numerical application still needs separate checks. Bad generation/evaluation law transfer, insufficient C/coverage power, coarse G-grid, model/law information privilege and resource limits remain separate diagnoses. The RISK/COVERAGE/precision bounds and status taxonomy do not constitute a new XAI algorithm. H1b completion robustness and H2 risk questions are not merged.
