Existing report elsewhere acknowledges these facts; the concern is the unqualified PASS label in the prespecified-threshold table. Keep CONDITIONAL GO parent-owned.

3. **Correct the generic AMAE sentence in Y1_NOVELTY_AND_POSITIONING.md.** AMAE is an equally class-weighted average absolute ordinal distance; minimizing validation AMAE does not ensure a decrease in held-out AMAE or either endpoint recall. Replace the sentence saying AMAE reduces that quantity with its definition and distinguish selector objective from realized test effect. The immediately following SkillCraft RF counterexample and the results table themselves show held-out nAMAE increases by 0.000897, so a broad reduction statement is misleading even though the surrounding caveat is sound.
