## Interpretation and limitations

This is a post-v1, source-audit amendment restricted to leagues 1–7, frozen prospectively for this pilot; it is not an untouched out-of-sample confirmation or an automatic GO. Exclusion of league 8 addresses documented heterogeneous professional acquisition, not all residual source heterogeneity. The source protocol describes one replay per participant, but exact UCI-paper cohort linkage and person identities are not authenticated. GameID uniqueness and duplicate-predictor grouping cannot establish independent individuals: multiple accounts, shared opponents/matches or other latent dependencies may remain.

Only 35 high-class examples/groups support the endpoint. All-class support and zero unsupported bootstrap draws do not establish precision, calibration or reliable tail performance. The group bootstrap resamples fixed predictions conditional on fitted models and presumes independent groups; it does not account for refitting, repeated-split dependence or hidden person/match links. Do not treat folds/repeats as independent scientific units or use this single pilot to assert cross-source significance, generalization to new populations, causal effects, or scientific confirmation. Report low/high endpoints separately, candidate/weight selections, reversals and costs; a validation recall constraint does not guarantee held-out recall.
