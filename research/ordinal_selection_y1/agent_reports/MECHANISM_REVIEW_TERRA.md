## Design and leakage

- Fixed train/validation/test draws are disjoint; the same underlying covariates, noise and membership are reused across cells within seed. Seeds, not folds or candidates, are the replicate units. Training pipelines fit once on train and PO balanced sample weights are derived from its training labels; RF uses training class weights. Population thresholds avoid sample-quantile leakage. Selection receives validation scores only, separately by family; test scores do not enter choose. No direct test-to-selection leakage is apparent on the inspected path.
- Implementation computes and stores every candidate test score before invoking selection. This is not computational leakage in the current code, but weakens procedural blinding: test results are available for human-guided protocol changes. Public reporting should disclose sequencing and avoid calling the test set inaccessible or independently sequestered. Freeze is prospective only after the earlier pilot, not study-wide preregistration. No pre-freeze timestamp verification beyond recorded metadata is claimed.
- The same random seed couples RF stochasticity across candidates but is not a guarantee of identical bootstrap samples or causal isolation of model randomness across differing training configurations. Fixed-hyperparameter paired contrasts isolate the specified algorithmic weight option with training/sample variation, not a real-world causal mechanism. In selected-policy contrasts, weights and hyperparameters may both change: attribution to either alone is invalid. Hard-label scores use argmax, so findings are conditional on that decoder, pool, split sizes and Gaussian latent-threshold DGP.
- Missing classes stop the run rather than being silently dropped; complete status implies no such recorded stop. A small rare validation class still causes noisy selector ranking. The qwk_recall floor is enforced on validation, not test or population recall; all reported infeasible/fallback counts are zero, so these results do not validate fallback behavior or guarantee held-out constraints.

## Inference and numerical findings

- Per-cell t intervals use ten paired seed-level differences, df=9, and cross-cell contrasts subtract matching seeds before aggregation. This is the correct pairing unit and common-random-number design, provided replicate streams are independent. Intervals target Monte Carlo expectations under this DGP including finite-train/validation/test and algorithm randomness, not uncertainty across real datasets or deployment populations. Ten replicates, bounded/skewed or zero-inflated differences and candidate-selection ties make normal/t approximations fragile. Intervals are unadjusted across cells, families, metrics and interactions; do not present selected nonzero intervals as confirmatory. Exact zero-width intervals, such as PO AMAE-minus-QWK endpoint recall in symrare signal 0.5, reflect no observed variation in these ten contrasts, not proof that the policy effect is identically zero. Crossing zero is not proof of equivalence.
- Primary AMAE-selected minus QWK-selected mean extreme recall is heterogeneous. In balanced signal 0.5 RF it is -0.0583, t95 [-0.1130, -0.0036], while in asym signal 2 PO it is +0.0199 [0.0041, 0.0357]. The latter combines low recall -0.0636 with high recall +0.1033: averaging endpoints hides opposing harms. In symrare signal 0.5 PO the contrast is exactly zero in all ten recorded replicates. These descriptive examples rebut a universal AMAE benefit or universal QWK harm within this experiment, not establish general superiority of either policy.
- Under symrare, all 10 QWK and AMAE selections use balanced weighting for both families at both signal levels. Similar selected weight frequencies do not prove identical candidate identities; report actual same-candidate rate before explaining small effects. In asym signal 2, PO QWK selects balanced 4/10 and AMAE 10/10; selection-metric differences there can coincide with training-weight choice and cannot be isolated from it by policy contrasts alone.
- Fixed-hyperparameter controls show weighting itself can substantially change endpoints and reverse sign: symrare signal 0.5 PO h0 balanced-minus-none mean extreme recall +0.5080 [0.4910, 0.5250], whereas asym signal 0.5 RF h1 is -0.0801 [-0.0927, -0.0674]. These are descriptive MC contrasts for different configurations, not proof that class weighting causes the between-cell difference; use precomputed paired interaction intervals for interaction claims.
