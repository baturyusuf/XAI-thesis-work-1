# Phase2 publication evidence scopes

- GIT_READ_MANIFEST.csv identifies immutable A3/A4/A5 and prior A1 blobs with honest reading-depth fields. These inputs were read, not rerun.
- PRIMARY_EXTRACTIONS.json and PRIMARY_PASSAGE_CHECKS.json hold method/object/target/assumption/guarantee/code/comparability extractions and short located snippets. They are not independent experiment results.
- NATIVE_BAYES_SCORE_CHECK.json records the critical native score/source lines and SHA. It is a limited source quote, not redistributed third-party implementation.
- Selected *_primary_access.json and author-code metadata retain access status, URL/hash and scope. LICENSE404, UNVERIFIED pins/equivalence and abstract-only SLICE remain unresolved.
- DELIVERABLE_MANIFEST.csv records the original8files before publication notices. VALIDATION_REPORT.json records the earlier local review checks, including query snapshots excluded from this smaller publication. Their old counts/hashes are historical and are not falsely reused for this bundle.
- PUBLICATION_WHITELIST.txt, PUBLICATION_MANIFEST.csv and PUBLICATION_VALIDATION.json define the final publication. The manifest excludes only itself, records staged blob and local byte hashes separately, and includes the whitelist. Its own content has a separate exact index/working comparison.
- PUBLICATION_EXCLUSIONS.csv identifies unneeded discovery snapshots/log payloads deliberately not staged. SEARCH_LOG preserves successful/failed and agent-reported access limits. .cache remains ignored, holds fulltexts/source downloads with possible public author contact information, and is NEVER part of publication.
- These records do not establish complete2026coverage, model fidelity, native performance, Phase2 design acceptance or algorithmic novelty. Current Agent5 gates are attributed to the human message in PUBLICATION_SAFETY_REVIEW, not an unexamined new source commit.
