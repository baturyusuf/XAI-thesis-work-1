# Publication timing evidence

Intervals below are calendar-day differences from the dates in the extraction notes. Received-to-accepted uses only records with both article-history dates. Accepted-to-published uses only cases with a documented first/online or version-of-record date. Where only an issue or generic Crossref/publisher date is known, no online interval is inferred. Revised dates were not exposed in the supplied notes for the included papers. Special-issue status is unknown unless the note explicitly establishes it; no special-issue extrapolation is made.

## Interpretation and limits

These are descriptive intervals for a purposively selected, proximity-focused collection of already published papers—not a representative journal sample, a forecast, or evidence of editorial speed. The sample is conditioned on publication/acceptance and has substantial selection bias. The underlying paper sets were not assembled by random sampling. Missing received/accepted fields reduce and may bias the denominators; N=0 means no computable pair in the notes, not a zero-day interval. Median/range should not be compared across journals as performance measures.

For paper-level exact received, revised, accepted and published fields, see PAPER_TIMELINES.csv. Revised is NR/not exposed throughout. Published dates in that CSV preserve source wording and distinguish verified online/VOR dates from generic/issue dates. No dates were generalized from special issue, invited or conference material.
