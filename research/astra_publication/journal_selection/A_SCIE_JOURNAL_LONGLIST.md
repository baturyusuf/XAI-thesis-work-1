## Evidence interpretation

All JCR edition, metric-year, category, rank/denominator and quartile cells are NR pending direct verification. Publisher index lists are publisher statements, not verified Clarivate records. Do not substitute JIF or SJR for JCR quartiles. JCR and MJL interfaces required JavaScript in the available fetch environment; no challenge was bypassed. CSV dates record the relevant check or Astra-supplied correction, not a guarantee that policies remain unchanged.

Springer hybrid pages explicitly identify a subscription route with no APC, but [Springer support](https://support.springer.com/en/support/solutions/articles/6000084580-costs-of-publishing-in-springer-nature-journal) warns that page and overlength charges may apply. Inspected Springer guidelines say online color is free and print color has a conditional charge, avoidable with monochrome print. Fee columns distinguish submission, processing, page, overlength, color and supplement. Optional OA, offprints, paid services, third-party permissions and ESWA co-submission are not mandatory subscription charges.

Springer figures are homepage-reported median submission-to-first-decision times, not acceptance times; cohort and desk-rejection treatment were not stated. ESWA timings are publisher labels and statistical definitions were not independently checked. No timing predicts the present manuscript outcome.
