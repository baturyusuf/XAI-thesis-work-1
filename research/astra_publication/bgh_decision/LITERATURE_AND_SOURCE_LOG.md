## Honest novelty position

Plausible question: under a documented next-year rating task, do macro-F1-versus-QWK selection and aggregate-versus-tail conclusions change when evaluation respects time and worker recurrence? BGH could test whether S1’s empirical observation extends or fails, rather than simply adding a new model/dataset pair. SHAP on an old dataset alone is not algorithmic novelty. Timing, missing-label selection and measurement changes are established general problems; their BGH-specific unresolved empirical implications require results, not a literature-negative claim.
